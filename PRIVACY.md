# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

Mosaikk is a macOS app for making photo collages, made by TangSpark. This policy covers every version of Mosaikk: the Mac App Store version and the one downloaded from tang-spark.no.

**Mosaikk collects no data.** It has no network access at all; the operating system's App Sandbox does not allow it to connect to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or update checks, and no third-party code.

What Mosaikk does on your Mac:

- It reads the pictures you add, to show them and put them in your collage.
- A collage is saved as a `.mosaikk` file where you choose. The file contains copies of the pictures you added, unchanged, so the collage can be opened and edited later, also on another Mac. Picture metadata, such as the date or location a photo was taken, stays in those copies; the exported image does not include it.
- Photos you add from your Photos library are chosen in the system's photo picker, so Mosaikk never has access to the library itself.
- It writes files only where you ask: the collage file and the images you export.

None of this leaves your Mac unless you share it yourself. Links in the Help menu open in your web browser, which is outside Mosaikk.
