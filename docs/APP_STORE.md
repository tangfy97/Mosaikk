# Releasing Mosaikk

Mosaikk ships two ways from the same code: the **Mac App Store** (the `AppStore` build configuration) and a **download on tang-spark.no** (the `Release` configuration, signed with Developer ID and notarized). Both run in the App Sandbox with no network entitlement.

## Every release

1. Bump `MARKETING_VERSION` (and for the App Store, `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION`) in `project.yml`, and add the release to `CHANGELOG.md`.
2. Check everything, and look at the snapshots:
   ```bash
   scripts/preflight.sh
   scripts/snapshots.sh light && scripts/snapshots.sh dark
   ```
3. App Store: build, then upload.
   ```bash
   scripts/appstore.sh upload
   ```
4. Website: build, sign, notarize and staple.
   ```bash
   scripts/release.sh
   ```

**Description**:

Mosaikk makes photo collages on your Mac.

Pick one of over 200 layouts, drop in your photos, and shape it by hand: drag the borders between photos to resize them, drag inside a photo to choose what shows, drag a photo onto another to swap them. Then add words, stickers and a background, and export a picture at any size.

LAYOUTS
• Grids, feature layouts and mosaics for 1 to 25 photos
• Circles, hearts, stars, hexagons, arches and pebbles
• Freeform: lay photos out loosely, like prints on a table
• Split cells, delete them, add more, or make them all even
• Spacing, margins, rounded corners and shadows

PHOTOS
• Crop by dragging, zoom, turn and flip
• 16 filters with adjustable strength
• Exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, tint, highlights, shadows, hue, vignette, sharpness and blur
• Fill empty cells or shuffle the photos with one click

TEXT AND STICKERS
• Any font on your Mac, with outline, shadow, glow and highlight
• Ready-made text styles, typed straight onto the collage
• Shapes, badges, decorations and emoji
• Move, resize, rotate, layer, lock and duplicate, with snapping

BACKGROUNDS AND SIZES
• Colors, gradients, patterns, a blurred photo, or transparent
• Square, portrait, story and wide; Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube, Pinterest and LinkedIn sizes; A4, Letter and photo prints; or any size you like

SAVE AND SHARE
• PNG, JPEG or HEIC, up to 12,000 pixels
• Collages are documents that keep your original photos, so you can come back and change them
• Copy, share, and undo anything

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your Mac. Mosaikk has no network access at all: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no watermark.

## Notes for App Review

> Mosaikk makes photo collages. It needs no account, has no in-app purchases, and has no network access.
>
> To try it: a new collage opens with a three-photo layout. Click a + in a cell, or choose File > Add Photos…, and pick a few pictures. Drag the line between two photos to resize them; drag inside a photo to move it, or onto another photo to swap. The sidebar on the left has layouts, photos, text styles, stickers and backgrounds; the inspector on the right changes whatever is selected. File > Export… saves the collage as an image.
>
> It runs in the App Sandbox with user-selected read-write access. Add from Photos uses the system photo picker, so no library permission is requested. The Help menu links open in the browser.
