# Privacy policy draft

Collage Atelier processes photos and collages locally on your Mac. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, or application network access. The developer does not receive your photos, documents, or usage data through the app.

The app embeds original photo data inside your editable .atelier project. Original metadata may remain in those source files. Exported PNG/JPEG collages are new images without copied source metadata. Copying places the rendered image on the macOS clipboard, where other applications may access it according to system permissions.

You control where documents and exports are saved. If you choose an iCloud Drive or other synced folder, that service manages synchronization under its own privacy terms. macOS may retain autosaves, versions, recent-document history, backups, or crash reports according to your system settings.

Appearance preferences are saved in the app's own local user defaults. They are not transmitted or shared with other apps.

Before publication, add the operator's legal identity, contact, effective date, and hosted public URL. This draft is not a live published policy.
