# Changelog

All notable changes to Mosaikk are listed here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

The first release, rebuilt from scratch as Mosaikk (previously the unreleased Collage Atelier).

- Over 200 layouts for 1 to 25 photos, shaped frames, templates with a floating frame, and freeform collages.
- Drag the borders between cells; aligned borders move together, ⌥ moves one. Split, delete, add and even out cells.
- Pan, zoom, turn and flip photos inside their cells; drag a photo onto another cell to swap; Auto Fill and Shuffle.
- 16 filters with strength, and 12 adjustments per photo.
- Text with installed fonts and effects (outline, shadow, glow, highlight), typed on the canvas; 14 text styles.
- Stickers: shapes, badges, decorations and emoji.
- A free layer with move, resize from all handles, rotate, snapping, opacity, lock, duplicate and arrange.
- Colour, gradient, pattern, blurred photo and transparent backgrounds.
- Canvas presets for proportions, social media and print, or a custom size.
- PNG, JPEG and HEIC export at any size, Copy Collage, and Share.
- `.mosaikk` documents that keep the original photos, with autosave, versions and undo.
- English, English (UK), Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
- Opens straight into a new collage; you choose where to keep it only when you save. Temporary files and autosaves of untitled collages that no window can reopen are cleaned up, so Mosaikk doesn't grow over time.
